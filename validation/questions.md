# Analytical questions

Questions a real Oakwell leadership team might ask of this warehouse. They
are grouped by audience and tagged by difficulty. Ground-truth answers for a
verified subset live in `ground_truth.json` / `ground_truth.md`.

The warehouse as-of date is **31 August 2026**.

## Founder / CEO

1. **Easy.** What was collected revenue last month (July 2026)?
2. **Easy.** What is ending MRR as of August 2026, and how many customers does that represent?
3. **Medium.** How has ending MRR changed each month over the last six months?
4. **Medium.** Which customer segments contribute the most ARR today?
5. **Hard.** If revenue declined in a region, was that cash timing (annual invoices), logo churn, or contraction?

## Finance / CFO

6. **Easy.** What is ending ARR as of August 2026?
7. **Easy.** How much of July 2026 collected revenue came from the United States?
8. **Medium.** What was net new MRR in August 2026, and how did the bridge (new, expansion, contraction, churn, reactivation) add up?
9. **Medium.** What is the average paid invoice value in July 2026, and how does it compare to the median?
10. **Medium.** How much collected revenue in 2026 came from invoices of $2,000 or more?
11. **Hard.** How much of billed cash is subscription versus add-on versus one-time implementation?
12. **Hard.** What is outstanding on open invoices, and is that concentrated in recent invoices or aged receivables?

## Revenue operations

13. **Easy.** How much new MRR landed in August 2026, and how many new paying customers was that?
14. **Medium.** Which acquisition channels hold the most ending MRR among currently active customers?
15. **Medium.** Do self-serve customers contribute a material share of ending MRR, or is the book still sales-led?
16. **Hard.** Which channels produce the highest-ARPU customers once they are active?
17. **Hard.** Among customers who expanded this quarter, which original acquisition channel and segment are they in?

## Sales leadership

18. **Easy.** How many paying organisations signed up in 2025 versus 2026 year to date?
19. **Medium.** Which countries added the most new customers in the first half of 2026?
20. **Medium.** What is trial-to-paid conversion for trials started in 2025, and does it differ by region?
21. **Hard.** Which plans are new logos landing on, and how often do they upgrade within two quarters?

## Product

22. **Easy.** How many work items were completed in August 2026?
23. **Medium.** How does product usage (work items and seat utilisation) differ between currently active and currently churned customers?
24. **Medium.** Are high-usage customers the ones expanding, or is expansion happening on low-utilisation accounts buying seats ahead of adoption?
25. **Hard.** In the 45 days before churn, does daily active-seat utilisation drop relative to retained peers in the same segment?

## Customer success

26. **Easy.** Which customers expanded in August 2026 (movement type = expansion)?
27. **Medium.** Which plans have the highest logo churn in 2026?
28. **Medium.** Are Enterprise customers on annual terms materially less likely to churn than SMB monthly customers?
29. **Hard.** How does net revenue retention in August 2026 compare with gross MRR churn in the same month?

## Support leadership

30. **Easy.** How many tickets were opened in Q2 2026, and what is the category mix?
31. **Medium.** Are currently churned customers over-represented in billing and bug tickets relative to their share of the customer base?
32. **Medium.** What is p90 first-response time, and does it differ for high-priority tickets?
33. **Hard.** Do customers with more tickets in the prior 90 days churn at a higher rate the following month than customers with few or no tickets?

## Ground-truth coverage

Verified numeric answers exist for questions 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8 (components), 10, 11 (subscription revenue), 12, 13, 15, 20 (2025 conversion), 22, 26 (expansion MRR), and 30. See `ground_truth.md`.
